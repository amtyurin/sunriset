# Sunrise widget: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Sunrise widget ("the app") shows sunrise, sunset and twilight times on your home screen. It is published by Andrey Tyurin.

## Data the app uses

**Location.** You can enter coordinates by hand, or you can tap "Get current location" on the widget settings screen. When you tap that button, the app asks for approximate location permission. It reads your location one time, only while the settings screen is open. The app never reads your location in the background.

The coordinates are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app uses them only to calculate sunrise and sunset times for the widget.

## Data the app does not collect or share

- The app does not send any data off your device. It has no internet permission.
- The app does not share data with third parties.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.
- The app does not use Android backup, so its settings are not copied to cloud storage.

## Deleting your data

Remove the widget from your home screen to delete its saved coordinates. Uninstall the app to delete all of its data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
